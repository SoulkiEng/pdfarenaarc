**PDFArenaArc** ([https://pdfarenaarc.com](https://pdfarenaarc.com)) is a 100% free, zero-upload, browser-based PDF utility suite powered by **Client-Side WebAssembly (Wasm)** and HTML5 APIs. Every document operation—merging, splitting, compressing to exact KB limits, redacting, and signing—executes entirely inside your local browser RAM. **Your files never leave your computer.**

## 🛠️ Complete Suite of 17 Core Client-Side PDF Utilities

### Organize & Optimize
- **[Merge PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/merge-pdf)** — Combine multiple PDF files into one single document in seconds.
- **[Split PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/split-pdf)** — Extract individual pages or custom page ranges into independent PDFs.
- **[Compress PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/compress-pdf)** — Reduce PDF file size while preserving vector text sharpness.
- **[Organize PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/organize-pdf)** — Reorder, sort, delete, and rotate PDF pages visually.
- **[Grayscale PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/grayscale-pdf)** — Convert color PDFs to black-and-white to save ink and shrink byte size.

### Convert To & From PDF
- **[PDF to Word](https://pdfarenaarc.com/pdf-to-word)** — Convert PDF documents into editable Word (`.docx`) files locally.
- **[Word to PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/word-to-pdf)** — Convert `.docx` documents into clean, print-ready PDFs.
- **[JPG to PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/jpg-to-pdf)** — Combine JPG, PNG, and WebP images into a single PDF album.
- **[PDF to JPG](https://pdfarenaarc.com/pdf-to-jpg)** — Render PDF pages into high-resolution JPG or PNG images.
- **[PDF to Text](https://pdfarenaarc.com/pdf-to-text)** — Extract raw text and paragraphs from PDF files instantly.

### Edit, Sign & Security
- **[Sign PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/sign-pdf)** — Draw, type, or upload your electronic signature directly in-browser.
- **[Redact PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/redact-pdf)** — Permanently black out SSNs, account numbers, and confidential text.
- **[Protect PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/protect-pdf)** — Encrypt PDF files with strong password protection.
- **[Unlock PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/unlock-pdf)** — Remove password restrictions from authorized PDF documents.
- **[Rotate PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/rotate-pdf)** — Rotate upside-down or sideways pages by 90°, 180°, or 270°.
- **[Watermark PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/watermark-pdf)** — Stamp custom text watermarks (`CONFIDENTIAL`, `DRAFT`) across pages.
- **[Add Page Numbers](https://pdfarenaarc.com/page-numbers)** — Insert clean pagination into multi-page PDF packets.

## 🔒 Privacy Guarantee

Unlike traditional online PDF converters that upload your documents to remote cloud storage buckets, **PDFArenaArc** executes 100% locally in your web browser. Once the application loads, you can disconnect from the internet and continue processing documents offline with zero data transmission.
