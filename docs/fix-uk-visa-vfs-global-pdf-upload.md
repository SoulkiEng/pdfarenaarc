# How to Compress & Format PDFs for UK Visa (UKVI, VFS Global & TLScontact)

When uploading supporting evidence for a **UK Standard Visitor Visa**, **Skilled Worker Visa**, **Student Visa**, or **Spouse Visa (Appendix FM)** via **VFS Global** or **TLScontact**, applicants frequently encounter upload timeouts, `"File too large"` errors (5MB limit), or `"Encrypted / Password-Protected PDF Not Allowed"` rejections.

---

## 🛠️ How to Fix Common VFS Global & TLScontact PDF Errors Locally

### 1. Shrink Multi-Page Scans Under 5MB Without Blurring Text
Use **[Compress PDF for UK Visa (UKVI / VFS Tool)](https://pdfarenaarc.com/compress-pdf-for-uk-visa/)**. It executes 100% in your local browser via WebAssembly to reduce 15MB–30MB scanned tenancy agreements, bank statements, and sponsor packets down under **5MB** (and even under 2MB) for instant VFS Global uploads.

### 2. Remove Bank Statement Passwords Before Uploading to VFS
VFS Global and TLScontact document scanners automatically reject password-protected e-statements. Use **[Unlock PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/unlock-pdf/)** locally in your browser to strip the password restriction before uploading.

### 3. Convert Color Scans to Black & White (As Recommended by UKVI)
UKVI guidance recommends black-and-white/grayscale document uploads for faster processing and smaller file sizes. Use **[Grayscale PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/grayscale-pdf/)** to convert color scans into crisp grayscale PDFs locally.
