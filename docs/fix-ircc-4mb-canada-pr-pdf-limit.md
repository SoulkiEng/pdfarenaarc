# How to Combine & Compress PDFs Under 4MB for IRCC Canada PR, Express Entry & Study Permits

Applicants submitting documents to **Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)** for **Express Entry (PR)**, **Study Permits**, **PGWP**, or **Spousal Sponsorship** face two strict technical constraints:

1. **The Single-Slot Rule**: IRCC only provides **one upload slot** per category (e.g., *Proof of Means of Financial Support*, *Client Information*, or *Employment Reference Letters*). You must combine dozens of pages into a single PDF file.
2. **The Strict 4MB Limit**: Once merged, the combined PDF must be **strictly under 4MB (4,096 KB)** or the IRCC portal rejects the upload.

---

## ✅ Step-by-Step 100% Private Workflow (Zero Cloud Uploads)
