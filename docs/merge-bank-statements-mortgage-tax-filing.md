# How to Merge Bank Statements, W-2s & 1099s for Mortgage Underwriting & CPA Tax Portals (100% Local Privacy)

Mortgage underwriters (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA, VA loans) and CPA tax portals require consecutive monthly bank statements, pay stubs, W-2s, and 1099s combined into clean, chronological PDF packets.

However, uploading unredacted bank statements and tax returns to free cloud PDF websites creates a severe identity-theft and data-breach risk.

---

## 🔒 The 100% Client-Side Mortgage & Tax PDF Workflow

With **[PDFArenaArc](https://pdfarenaarc.com/)**, every byte is processed locally inside your browser's WebAssembly memory—**zero files are ever uploaded to any external server**.

### 1. Merge Monthly Bank Statements for Mortgage Underwriting
Use **[Merge Bank Statements for Mortgage](https://pdfarenaarc.com/merge-bank-statements-for-mortgage/)**:
- Combine 2 to 12 months of checking, savings, and brokerage PDF statements in chronological order.
- Preserves all pages (including `"Intentionally Left Blank"` pages required by mortgage underwriters so your packet is never flagged for missing pagination).

### 2. Combine W-2, 1099 & Receipts for CPA Tax Filing
Use **[Merge PDF for Tax Filing](https://pdfarenaarc.com/merge-pdf-for-tax-filing/)**:
- Merge W-2s, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-INT, K-1s, and deductible expense receipts into one organized audit-ready PDF.

### 3. Redact Sensitive Account Numbers (When Permitted)
Need to black out unrelated transaction lines or SSNs before sharing documents with third parties? Use **[Redact PDF](https://pdfarenaarc.com/redact-pdf/)** to permanently burn redaction boxes into the document locally.
