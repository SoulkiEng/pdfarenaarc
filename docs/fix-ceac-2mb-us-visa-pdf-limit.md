# How to Fix US Visa CEAC & USCIS 2MB PDF File Size Limit Error (Zero Cloud Uploads)

If you are uploading civil documents, I-864 Affidavits of Support, IRS Tax Transcripts, or multi-page passport scans to the **U.S. Department of State Consular Electronic Application Center (CEAC)** or **USCIS myUSCIS**, you have likely encountered the strict error:

> **"The file size must be 2MB (2,048 KB) or less."**

---

## ⚠️ Why You Should Never Use Cloud PDF Compressors for US Visa Documents

Your DS-260, I-864, and USCIS packets contain your most sensitive personally identifiable information (PII):
- **U.S. Social Security Numbers (SSN) & ITINs**
- **Passport Numbers & Biometric Pages**
- **IRS Form 1040 / W-2 / 1099 Tax Transcripts**
- **Full Bank Account Routing & Statement Histories**

Traditional online PDF compressors upload your files to remote third-party cloud servers, exposing your immigration and financial records to external storage buckets.

---
